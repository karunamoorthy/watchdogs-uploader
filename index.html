Watchdogs Uploader

A private tool that uploads short videos to the YouTube channels of its owner.

What it does

  • Uploads videos made by the owner to the owner's own YouTube channels (USA-Watchdogs and Europe-Watchdogs) and sets their title, description and publishing time.
  • Reads the view counts and status of those videos, so the owner can see how they perform.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the channels. The tool runs on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Google account access

The tool asks for access to YouTube so it can upload and manage videos on the owner's channels and read their statistics. It uses that access for nothing else. See the privacy policy.

Watchdogs Uploader is a personal project. It is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.
